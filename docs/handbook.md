# Northwind Robotics — Employee Handbook

## 1. Working Hours and Flexibility

Northwind Robotics operates on a flexible schedule. Core collaboration hours are
10:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone, during which you are expected to be
reachable for meetings and pair work. Outside those hours you may arrange your
day as you see fit, provided your commitments are met.

The standard work week is 40 hours. Overtime is not expected and is not tracked.
If you consistently find yourself working beyond 45 hours in a week, raise it
with your manager — that is treated as a planning failure, not a personal one.

## 2. Paid Leave

Employees accrue paid leave at a rate of 1.75 days per completed month of
service, which works out to 21 days per calendar year. Accrual begins on your
first day; there is no waiting period.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal to your direct manager at
least 14 days in advance. Requests made inside the 14-day window require
approval from both your manager and your department head, and may be declined
if they conflict with a release window.

Unused leave carries over to the following year up to a maximum of 5 days. Any
balance above 5 days on 31 December is forfeited. We do not pay out unused
leave except on termination of employment.

Sick leave is separate from paid leave and is not capped. A doctor's note is
required only for absences exceeding three consecutive working days.

## 3. Remote Work

All engineering roles are remote-eligible. Employees may work from any location
within a time zone that permits attendance during core collaboration hours,
without prior approval.

Working from a country other than your country of employment requires written
approval from HR at least 30 days in advance, because it creates tax and payroll
obligations for the company. Approval is normally granted for stays under 60
days. Stays beyond 90 days in a foreign jurisdiction are not permitted under any
circumstances.

The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $1,200, claimable after
completing your probation period. It may be spent on a desk, chair, monitor, or
other ergonomic equipment. Receipts are required and the equipment remains your
property.

## 4. Expenses and Reimbursement

Expenses under $200 do not require pre-approval. Submit the receipt through the
finance portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Reimbursements are paid with
the next payroll run following approval.

Expenses of $200 or more require written pre-approval from your manager before
the purchase is made. Retroactive approval is not granted; an unapproved
expense above the threshold will be declined regardless of merit.

Travel is booked through the company travel desk. Economy class is standard for
flights under six hours; premium economy is permitted for longer flights.
Business class requires VP approval. Accommodation is capped at $250 per night
in most cities, and $350 per night in San Francisco, New York, London, Zurich,
and Tokyo.

Client entertainment is reimbursable up to $75 per head. Alcohol is reimbursable
only when part of a client meal.

## 5. Equipment and Security

Every employee receives a company laptop. Personal devices may not be used to
access production systems, customer data, or the internal code repositories
under any circumstances.

Full-disk encryption is mandatory and is enforced by our device management
software. Laptops automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity; this setting
cannot be changed by the user.

Passwords must be at least 16 characters. Multi-factor authentication is
required on all company accounts without exception. We use hardware security
keys for production access; SMS-based codes are not accepted for any system.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to security@northwind.example within
one hour of discovery, regardless of the time of day. The device will be remotely
wiped immediately. There is no penalty to the employee for a lost device that is
reported promptly.

## 6. Probation and Reviews

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. During probation either
party may terminate the employment with 1 week of notice. After probation the
notice period is 8 weeks for both parties.

Performance reviews are held twice yearly, in March and September. Compensation
adjustments are made once yearly, effective 1 April, and are decided in the
March review cycle.

Promotion is not tied to the review calendar. A promotion case may be raised by
your manager at any time and is decided by the department head with HR present.
